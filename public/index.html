Our story

Takeaway pizza deserves better.

Rocco started the pizzeria with a simple conviction: takeaway pizza has copped an unfair fast-food reputation, and it doesn't have to be that way.

So the pizzas here follow the principles of the Mediterranean diet: a sourdough base, lots of vegetables, good olive oil and honest toppings.

“Slow-fermented dough, imported tomatoes, fresh basil. No shortcuts. No unnecessary extras.”
