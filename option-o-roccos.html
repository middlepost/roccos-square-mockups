About Rocco's

No shortcuts.

For over 25 years, Rocco has trained in Italy and with Italian Masters to learn the art of pizza. At Rocco’s that now means slow-fermented dough, big Italian ovens, Mutti Italian tomatoes, imported mozzarella, sauces made from scratch and fresh Italian sausage (made in-house). No shortcuts - No unnecessary extras.
— Rocco DeAngelis
