The Story

It started with Mrs. Rocco

When Mrs. Rocco developed a food sensitivity that made her give up pizza, Rocco went to work. He crafted a sourdough mother from scratch, inoculated the dough, and fermented it for days until pizza was back on her table.

Years of training in Italy, hi-tech Italian ovens imported to Adelaide, a pizzeria on Findon Road and a sourdough lab on Grange Road later — the mission hasn't changed.

"You're only as good as your last pizza."
