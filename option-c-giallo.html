OUR STORY

Blame Mrs. Rocco 🍕

When Mrs. Rocco's food sensitivity took pizza off her plate, Rocco built a sourdough mother from scratch and fermented his dough for days until she could eat pizza again — light, delicious, easy to digest.

Trained for years in Italy, back in Adelaide with hi-tech Italian ovens: a pizzeria on Findon Road and a sourdough lab on Grange Road.
