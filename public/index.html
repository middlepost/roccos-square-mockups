Our story

Takeaway pizza deserves better.

Rocco started the pizzeria with a simple conviction: takeaway pizza has copped an unfair fast-food reputation, and it doesn't have to be that way.

So the pizzas here follow the principles of the Mediterranean diet: a sourdough base, lots of vegetables, good olive oil and honest toppings.

“Slow-fermented dough, imported tomatoes, fresh basil. No shortcuts. No unnecessary extras.”
Rocco’s promise

FAQs

Good questions

How does pickup work?

Tick the pizzas you want on the menu, choose Findon Rd or Grange Rd, pick a pickup time and pay by card. Pickup times run every 15 minutes from 5:30pm.

Can I order by phone in my language?

Yes. Call our AI phone line and order in the language you're most comfortable with.

Do you deliver?

This site is pickup only. For delivery, use the delivery apps.

Can I bring wine?

Yes, BYO with no corkage when you dine in. Beer and wine we sell are dine-in only.

Do you sell dough?

Yes. Ask at either shop or call ahead and we'll put some aside.

Allergies?

Some items contain nuts. Tell us in the order notes, or call the shop to check.
