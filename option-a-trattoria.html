Our Story

Why sourdough? Blame Mrs. Rocco.

When Mrs. Rocco developed a food sensitivity that made her give up pizza, Rocco went looking for an answer. He crafted his “mother” from scratch, inoculated the dough, and fermented it for days. The result was light, delicious, and easy to digest — pizza she could love again.

As a teen in Rome, Rocco ate pizza most days for pocket change — simple and delicious. After training in Italy for years, he returned to Adelaide, imported hi-tech Italian equipment, and opened on Findon Road, followed by his sourdough production lab on Grange Road.

Many say ours is the only pizza they can eat without bloating — or that it's so light, they could eat two.
